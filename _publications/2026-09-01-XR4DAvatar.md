![XR4DAvatar overview: system pipeline, reconstruction algorithm, and avatar comparison](/images/xr4davatar.jpg)

We present a novel framework for reconstructing photorealistic 4D avatars from monocular videos for XR applications. Unlike existing 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS)-based avatar reconstruction methods that rely on constrained human poses and camera setups and often lack animation rigs, our approach supports unconstrained real-world monocular videos as input and produces animatable, photorealistic avatars. We integrate a diffusion-based image generation model to generate multi-view images and a textured SMPL-X-guided 3D Gaussian Splatting model to construct 3D avatars. Our results show comparable visual quality to existing approaches while supporting direct animation. We further demonstrate a practical application of reconstructed avatars by directly driving 3DGS avatars with SMPL-based animation in Unity for both VR and AR.
